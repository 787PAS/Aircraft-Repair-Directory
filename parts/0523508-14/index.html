About this part

A wing or control-surface rib for the Cessna 150 at Sta 58. Ribs maintain the aerodynamic cross-section of the wing, transfer aerodynamic loads to the spars, and provide attachment points for the wing skin. Inspect for cracks at the lightening holes and at attachment flanges; corrosion in the internal cavity is common and must be treated before reassembly.
