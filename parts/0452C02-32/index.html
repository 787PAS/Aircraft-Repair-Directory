About this part

A airframe skin panel (right-hand, lower) used on the Cessna 150. Aircraft skin panels are load-bearing sheet-metal components that form both the aerodynamic outer surface and part of the primary structure. Inspect carefully for cracks, corrosion, dents, and improper previous repairs before installation; any skin damage must be assessed per the aircraft service manual.
